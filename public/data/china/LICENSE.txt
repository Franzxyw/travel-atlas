when redistributing this database or a derivative database. The publicly
available JSON bundles and crosswalk provide the derived database in an open,
machine-readable form; scripts/prepare-china.mjs describes its construction.

Province geometries: geoBoundaries / Wikimedia Commons, CHN-ADM1-43563684
(2019 snapshot). Underlying source marked Public Domain by geoBoundaries.

Name/code/parent/point catalog: zhChuXiao/ChinaGeoJson, revision
7aa2c5fabc6a892015625898c3482874b233f9de, MIT. Only factual catalog fields are
used; NO DataV geometries are bundled. The upstream MIT notice is reproduced
in CATALOG-LICENSE.txt.

Source URLs, pinned revisions and SHA-256 hashes: manifest.json.
Per-county identity matching and omitted source/catalog rows: crosswalk.json.

Changes: transliteration + centre/centroid containment matching; explicit
Luhe/Minhang/Duchang aliases; simplify to 0.0007 degrees; round to 5 decimal
places; group verified counties under catalog parents; dissolve matched
county polygons to form prefecture FOOTPRINTS. These are not authoritative
prefecture boundaries. Matching failures are omitted, not guessed.

These are historical, incomplete snapshots. Names, codes, parents and borders
may have changed; a matching code does not establish present-day geometry.
No personal visit records, notes or inferred visits are included in this data.
